## 1. What is RoleSync?

Applying for jobs today can feel like a full-time job. Most companies use automated software called **Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS)** that scan your resume before a human ever reads it. If your resume does not highlight the specific skills and keywords mentioned in the job description, it gets filtered out.

RoleSync solves this in three simple ways:
1. **Instant Resume Tailoring**: It re-organizes and highlights your *authentic* past work so it speaks the exact language of the job posting. It **never fabricates or invents** skills or numbers you don't actually have.
2. **1-Click Application Filling**: It types in your details, selects dropdown options, answers common screening questions, and automatically attaches your tailored resume.
3. **Direct Recruiter Outreach**: It finds the hiring manager behind the job and drafts a polite, concise, 100-word introduction email you can send with one click.

## 2. Quick 3-Minute Setup

Getting started takes less than 3 minutes.

### Step 1: Install the Chrome Extension
1. Open Google Chrome.
2. In the top-right corner of Chrome, click the **Puzzle Piece icon** (Extensions) and select **Manage Extensions**.
3. Turn on **Developer mode** in the top right.
4. Click **Load unpacked** and select the `RoleSync/extension/dist` folder.
5. Click the **Pin icon** next to RoleSync so it stays visible in your browser toolbar.

### Step 2: Set Up Your Master Profile
Your **Master Profile** is your master career vault. You only need to set it up once:
- **Contact Info**: Your name, email, phone number, location, and LinkedIn profile.
- **Career History**: All the roles you have held, company names, dates, and bullet points describing what you achieved.
- **Skills Vault**: All the tools, technologies, and skills you know.

> **Why a Master Profile?**  
> RoleSync uses your Master Profile as the sole source of truth. It will never claim you worked somewhere you didn't or used a tool you haven't listed.

## 3. How to Apply for a Job with RoleSync

Here is the exact day-to-day workflow you will use:

```
1. Browse to any job post (LinkedIn, Greenhouse, Lever, Workday)
   ↓
2. RoleSync side panel opens automatically and reads the job description
   ↓
3. See your Match Score (e.g., 88% Match)
   ↓
4. Click "Tailor Resume" → Get a tailored 1-page PDF in 5 seconds
   ↓
5. Click "Autofill Application" → Form fields fill in automatically
   ↓
6. Review your application and click "Submit"
```

### Step-by-Step Instructions:

1. **Find a Job You Like**:
   Browse to any job opening on **LinkedIn**, **Greenhouse**, **Lever**, **Workday**, or any company career page.
2. **Open RoleSync**:
   Click the RoleSync icon in your browser toolbar (or let the side panel open automatically). RoleSync instantly reads the job description from the page.
3. **Check Your Match Score**:
   RoleSync displays an overall score out of 100% showing how well your background matches what the employer is asking for.
4. **Click "Tailor Resume"**:
   In about 3 to 5 seconds, RoleSync compiles a customized, beautifully formatted resume tailored specifically for this role.
5. **Preview Your Resume**:
   You can view the full PDF right inside the side panel to ensure you are 100% happy with how it looks. It is guaranteed to fit neatly onto **exactly one page**—no messy overflow or blank space.
6. **Click "Autofill"**:
   RoleSync fills out the company's application form on your screen, types your answers naturally, and attaches your newly tailored PDF resume.
7. **Submit**:
   Do a quick final review with your own eyes, then click the company's "Submit Application" button.

## 4. Understanding Your ATS Match Score

When RoleSync scans a job description, it shows you a circular score card broken down into 4 clear categories:

| Category | What it Means in Plain English |
|:---|:---|
| **Hard Skill Match** | Do you have the specific tools, languages, or software the job requires? (e.g., Python, SQL, Salesforce). |
| **Experience Match** | Does your number of years in the field match what the employer is looking for? |
| **Keyword Relevance** | How well does your past terminology match the exact phrases used by the hiring team? |
| **Semantic Match** | Does the core meaning of your past work align with the everyday duties of this new job? |

If your score is **75% or higher**, you have a strong likelihood of passing the company's automated screening.

## 5. The Two Ways to Apply: Copilot vs. Autopilot

RoleSync gives you two ways to work, depending on how much control you want:

### Mode 1: Copilot Mode (Recommended for Most People)
- **How it works**: You are in the driver's seat. RoleSync suggests what to fill and generates your tailored resume, but waits for you to click "Autofill".
- **Best for**: Standard 1-page job applications (like Greenhouse or Lever) where you want to review each field before moving forward.

### Mode 2: Autopilot Mode (Great for Long, Multi-Step Portals like Workday)
- **How it works**: Some corporate portals (especially Workday) force you to click through 5 separate web pages:
  1. *Step 1: My Information* (Your name, address, phone number)
  2. *Step 2: My Experience* (Attaching resume, adding past jobs, education)
  3. *Step 3: Application Questions* (Work authorization, sponsorship, notice period)
  4. *Step 4: Voluntary Disclosures* (Equal opportunity and diversity surveys)
  5. *Step 5: Review & Submit*
- In Autopilot mode, RoleSync handles the tedious clicking between these pages automatically.
- **Safety First**: RoleSync will **always stop and pause at Step 5 (Review)**. It will never hit the final "Submit" button without your eyes on it and your explicit consent.

## 6. Reaching Out to Hiring Managers

Applying online is only half the battle. Reaching out directly to the person hiring can 10x your interview rate. RoleSync makes this effortless and polite:

1. **Find Recruiter**: In the side panel, click **Find Hiring Team**. RoleSync identifies the technical recruiter or department manager responsible for the role.
2. **Email Verification**: Before you send anything, RoleSync verifies the email address with official business directories so you know it won't bounce.
3. **Draft Generated**: RoleSync writes a warm, professional, concise email:
   - **Under 120 words** (hiring managers are busy; short emails get read).
   - Highlights 1 or 2 of your key achievements relevant to their open position.
   - Includes your contact details and a 1-click link for them to opt out if they prefer.
4. **Send with 1 Click**: Read the draft, make any personal edits you'd like, and send!

> **Anti-Spam Safety**: To protect your professional reputation, RoleSync caps automated outreach at a maximum of 15 emails per day so your messages always land in the primary inbox, never in the spam folder.

## 7. Frequently Asked Questions

#### Q: Does RoleSync lie or make up fake experience on my resume?
**No. Absolutely not.** RoleSync has strict anti-hallucination guardrails built into its core. It will only re-phrase, re-order, and highlight real achievements that are already in your Master Profile. If you have never worked with a tool, RoleSync will never add it to your resume.

#### Q: Will the employer know I used AI?
No. RoleSync generates a clean, standard vector PDF document using traditional typography. It looks like a resume hand-crafted in professional desktop publishing software. During autofill, it types with natural timing so websites don't flag you as a bot.

#### Q: Why did my resume fit on exactly one page?
Recruiters typically spend less than 7 seconds reviewing a resume, and resumes with orphan lines on page 2 often get discarded. RoleSync automatically adjusts typography spacing so your resume fills one page.

#### Q: Can I edit the information before it's submitted?
**Yes, always.** You can edit any bullet point in the tailored resume preview, change any field on the website form, and tweak every outreach email before sending.

#### Q: Which job boards are supported?
RoleSync works on **LinkedIn**, **Greenhouse**, **Lever**, **Workday**, **iCIMS**, **Taleo**, and almost any standard company careers website.

## 8. Tips for the Best Results

1. **Be Thorough in Your Master Profile**: The more detail, numbers, and genuine accomplishments you put into your Master Profile initially, the better RoleSync can tailor your resume for diverse roles.
2. **Include Measurable Outcomes**: When writing your past bullet points, include real numbers where possible (e.g., *"increased sales by 25%"*, *"managed a team of 4"*, *"reduced page load time by 1.2s"*). RoleSync will use these exact figures to make you stand out.
3. **Apply Early**: When you see a job posted within the last 24–48 hours, use RoleSync to get your tailored application in immediately before the recruiter's inbox gets crowded.
